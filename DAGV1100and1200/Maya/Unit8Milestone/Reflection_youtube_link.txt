I honestly found the UV’s quite difficult, I absolutely think I learned a lot but there was a lot of struggle and learning. I would start by opening my object and immediately, looking for hidden faces. Truly making sure you get rid of your faces as you are building is VITAL. Then I would start evaluating which exactly UV it needed, after that I would take my best guesses as to which ones to open. Then I would look for the weird angles and make sure to merge verts. Then I would slice and dice till it looked cohesive, laid it out, then check it with the checkered mesh. Then it was just a matter of checking colors and coming to a conclusion. One thing I would constantly face was the issue with the verts not combining due to hidden faces and edges. It took a lot of merging and moving around but after a lot of experimenting I could get it to work. I also ran into an issue where I just had so many assets in one where I would have to go through each one very repetitively. I find mapping the UV’s first and THEN ctrl+D to duplicate would allow the same uv map to be there without having to redo it.
